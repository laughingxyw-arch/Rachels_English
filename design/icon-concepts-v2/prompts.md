## A — Listening instrument

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: high quality square app icon concept for an original-speech listening and repetition app. Create ONE icon only, full bleed square, front-facing orthographic view. Native Apple Weather / Compass level of meticulous optical layering and material realism, without copying those symbols. A beautifully resolved simple sculptural object, clear at 48 pixels, restrained depth, precise soft upper-left illumination, nuanced contact shadows, smooth rich materials. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, mockup phone, surrounding layout, or decorative sparkles. No excessive glow, no cheap gradient logo, no chunky toy rendering. Concept A, Precision listening instrument: midnight graphite full-square background. Large central circular acoustic instrument with a satin titanium thin outer rim, dark softly concave face under subtly reflective clear glass. Within the dial, a single elegant ivory sound waveform runs horizontally through the center, gently rising into three smooth rounded peaks, like a measured spoken phrase. A restrained amber needle and minimal widely spaced fine tick marks suggest attentive listening, not navigation. The circular instrument fills 76% of the tile; distinct refined layers of rim, glass, face and waveform, no compass cardinal directions. Elegant almost-black, silver and small warm amber accent. Precise balanced silhouette, convincing polished system icon finish.
